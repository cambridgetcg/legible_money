12. Recommendations

Critical (Before DevNet)

Wire vesting_rewards into app.go

Without this, zero tokens are emitted. No validator has any incentive to participate.

Fix tier MinStake decimals

Bonded: "1111000000" (correct) and Guardian: "11111000000" (correct) — verify 6-decimal format.

Configure ResearchTreasuryAddress

22% of all rewards go to unconfigured account. Module account suffices for DevNet.

High Priority (Before Testnet)

Rebalance block vs verification rewards

Current 11:1 ratio violates PoT philosophy. Target: 2:1 or 3:1.

Guardian risk/reward rebalancing

1044:1 ratio discourages participation. Consider increasing Guardian reward multiplier or adding governance rewards.

Challenge economics tuning

9% break-even may incentivize spam. Consider raising MinChallengeStake or reducing reward ratio.

Complete burn implementation

5% burn rate declared but only implemented at 4 of 11+ penalty sites.
