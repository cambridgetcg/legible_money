# Wyoming LLC Formation: Legible Protocol LLC

> **Goal**: Create a privacy-preserving legal entity for domain registration and project ownership.

---

## Why Wyoming?

| Feature | Wyoming | Delaware | California |
|---------|---------|----------|------------|
| **Member privacy** | ✅ Not public | ✅ Not public | ❌ Public |
| **No state income tax** | ✅ | ❌ (franchise tax) | ❌ |
| **Annual report cost** | $60/year | $300/year | $800/year min |
| **Formation cost** | $100 | $90 | $70 |
| **Asset protection** | ✅ Strong | ✅ Good | ❌ Weak |
| **Crypto-friendly** | ✅ Very | ✅ Yes | ⚠️ Regulated |

**Wyoming wins**: Cheapest to maintain, most private, crypto-friendly laws.

## Optional: Get an EIN (Free)

An EIN (Employer Identification Number) is like a social security number for your LLC. **Free from IRS.**

Useful if you:
- Open a business bank account
- Accept payments to the LLC
- Want extra separation from personal identity

```
Get it at: https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/apply-for-an-employer-identification-number-ein-online

Takes: 10 minutes
Cost: Free
```
