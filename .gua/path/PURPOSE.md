**Purpose**: Advance the Legible Money protocol from L2 Practitioner (reliable execution) to L3 Craftsman (creative-active) — where beauty emerges naturally, justice is architectural, and creative paths integrate into a coherent whole.

1. [ ] All 5 dimensions at L2+ (currently met)
2. [ ] Justice (J2/J5/J6) gates passing — ownership is structural, not patched
3. [ ] Beauty (B4) gate passing — elegance emerges from understanding, not applied

5. [ ] Creativity (X3/X5) gates passing — original paths built and integrated

7. [ ] Gate pass rate ≥ 90% across all dimensions

## The Tension
The project has two stacks (TS ~226K LOC, Go ~11.5K LOC) and the strategic decision is to build forward in Go. But the TS chain holds the truth — 4054 tests, 40 subsystems, deep architecture. The path to L3 runs through *bridging* these stacks, not choosing one.

## Out of Scope (for L3)

- Completing all 24 Go modules (bulk work ≠ depth)
